CONFIRMED DESIGN CHANGES
The sequence is sand filter → 5 µm PP filter → carbon / palm Biochar filter → UV → verification.
Component ⑥ is F-303 (PP); component ⑦ is F-302 (carbon / Biochar), consistently throughout.
The base design retains 65-inch media vessels and the 2.20 m open frame.
Posts are at the corners/rear; there is no overhead beam above the filter service zone.
CP-801 mounts on a side/rear post, and the cable tray runs behind the filters.
Keep the 2.46 m service envelope unobstructed; the required site clear height is at least 2.50 m.
Check indoor ceiling/beam heights. The outdoor inset shows a 2.80 m canopy underside.
An outdoor canopy must be above 2.50 m or removable over the filters for maintenance.
Shorter/wider 54-inch vessels (e.g. 1854 instead of 1665) remain a future option only:
head height about 1.68 m, service envelope about 2.18 m, within a closed 2.20 m frame.
The current arrangement has not been changed to that alternative.

CONCEPT NOTES
Competition concept — not approved for construction. Dimensions approximate, based on common commercial sizes.
Equipment, hydraulic duties, control setpoints and disinfection performance require detailed engineering.
T-701 is 2 m³ nominal; 1.60 × 1.20 × 1.15 m is the supplied external envelope, not a verified usable capacity.
XV-601 is spring-return fail-closed. XV-602 must support the depicted interlocked modes, including holding.
P-103 has selected, isolated backwash and reprocessing duties; these do not operate simultaneously.
The PP cartridge is upstream of carbon/Biochar per the revised brief; final particulate protection at UV is to be verified.
Local fixed controller rules govern irrigation without internet; platform AI may only make decisions more conservative.
