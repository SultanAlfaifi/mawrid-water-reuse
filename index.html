Smart decentralized water reuse

Every drop deserves a second purpose.

MAWRID is a safety-oriented concept that recovers source-separated ablution greywater, treats it with low-energy filtration and date-palm biochar, and verifies it before non-potable reuse.

CONCEPT · PRE-VALIDATION
The opportunity

Water quality should match water purpose.

Ablution water is commonly discharged after one light use while drinking-quality water serves nearby non-potable demand. MAWRID explores a safer, local second use.

01 · RECOVER

Separate at the source

Collect only defined ablution and selected handwashing drainage, excluding toilet, kitchen, and industrial wastewater.

02 · TREAT

Use only what is needed

Combine screening, granular filtration, tested date-palm biochar, and controlled disinfection for a specified end use.

03 · PROTECT

Fail toward non-reuse

If quality, disinfection, or sensor health cannot be verified, the reuse valve closes and water is diverted safely.
